Hazard Engine — Core Probability Functions
AE Onset Hazard
h(t) = I · f(t) / (1 − I · F(t−1))
I = cumulative incidence from rule set
f(t) = onset PDF (Normal, LogNormal, or Uniform)
F(t) = onset CDF
Each AE type has its own distribution parameters. Daily conditional probability given no prior onset captures the "surviving population" effect.
Mortality (Log-Hazard)
p(t) = exp(log(base) + w · log(HR))
Two-channel mortality: disease progression and treatment toxicity. Severity weight w scales how much the hazard ratio matters: w≈1 for critically ill, w≈0.15 for stable patients. Prevents multiplicative explosion from correlated risk factors.
Lab Values (OU Process)
X(t+1) = X(t) + θ(target − X(t)) + σ·Z
Ornstein-Uhlenbeck mean-reversion. Target is a weighted blend of baseline and AE-causal target (e.g., ANC ↓ during neutropenia). Active AEs shift the target; resolution restores it.
Observation Model
GT ≠ HR — AE exists before detection
Ground Truth vs Hospital Record separation. AEs are detected through 4 channels: scheduled visit, patient self-report, video call (DCA), ER visit. Detection probability depends on AE type, grade, and patient mood.
